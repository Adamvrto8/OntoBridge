A Mortgage Customer is a natural person who has been approved for a mortgage loan secured by residential property. The loan-to-value ratio (LTV) must not exceed 80% at origination. Monthly mortgage repayments are calculated based on the customer's net monthly income and existing debt obligations. If the debt-service-to-income ratio (DSTI) exceeds 45%, the mortgage application is automatically rejected.
